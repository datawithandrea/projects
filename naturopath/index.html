Our Objective

Helping you achieve the best health possible

Our objective is to help you achieve the best health possible, using safe and scientifically proven methods, involving both functional medicine and naturopathy.

Ultimately we look at each person and as an individual, as there are many differences and variables that make up each person. ie genetics, gender, body shape, health history, family history, lifestyle, work load etc. Once enough information is compiled, a comprehensive treatment plan is created to help address your health concerns.

To understand your condition, you must first understand your symptoms and why are they occurring. Symptoms work like alarm bells, indicating that an underlying problem has occurred below the surface. A common mistake practitioners make, is to treat the symptoms only. This approach may work temporarily, but not long-term, we class this as a “band aid approach.” A successful long-term solution, is one that treats the root causes and drivers of the disease.

All health conditions generally take time to manifest, they may take months or years and its the ongoing exposure to synthetic toxins, heavy metals, inflammation, oxidative stress, infections, imbalances, nutritional deficiencies, stress, hormonal imbalances, poor diet, poor lifestyle, leaky gut and genetic predispositions, that may potentially lead to dysfunction and disease in the body.

Thanks to the latest advancements in functional medicine lab testing, we have the technology and insight to identify the root causes and drivers of disease, eliminating clinical assumptions. The lab results provide the practitioner with essential detailed information about the individuals health, ultimately allowing the practitioner to create an holistic treatment plan, to correct the imbalances using a multi-faceted approach. Once these underlying issues are corrected, the body will naturally try to find a healthy equilibrium, thus promoting the process of healing to occur.

Specialised Functional Laboratory Testing
DOCTOR’S DATA HTEE TEST (hair, toxic, essential elements)
Which tests for heavy metal toxicity and mineral imbalances, using a hair sample. Great test for those experiencing neurological complaints, fatigue, chronic fatigue syndrome (CFS), autism, ADHD, cancer and so on. With respect to its contained elements, hair is essentially an excretory tissue rather than a functional tissue. Hair element analysis provides important information which, in conjunction with symptoms and other laboratory values, can assist with the early detection of physiological disorders associated with aberrations in essential and toxic element metabolism. As protein is synthesised in the hair follicle, elements are incorporated permanently into the hair with no further exchange or equilibration with other tissues. Scalp hair is easy to sample, and because it grows an average of one to two cm per month, it contains a “temporal record” of element metabolism and exposure to toxic elements.
PRECISION ANALYTICAL D.U.T.C.H TEST (dried urine comprehensive hormone profile test)
Which is the most advance and comprehensive hormone test available. Great for woman who are experiencing ongoing hormonal dysfunction, weight-gain, reproductive issues, irregular periods, PCOS, recurring ovarian cysts, reproductive cancers, etc. This advanced hormone testing was developed to surpass industry standards for available testing options. This test looks at sex hormones and their metabolites, the overall diurnal pattern of free cortisol, and the total and distribution of cortisol metabolites in addition to OATs which provide insight into nutritional deficiencies, oxidative stress, gut dysbiosis, melatonin, neuro-inflammation and more. Such a comprehensive analysis provides practitioners and their patients with detailed information, putting a spotlight on areas of imbalance or concern, and helps to guide towards more positive treatment outcomes.
DIAGNOSTIC SOLUTIONS GI MAP TEST (comprehensive stool analysis and intestinal health markers)
This stool analysis can help to identify candida, yeast, fungi, viruses, parasites, worms, dysbiotic bacteria, healthy bacteria, inflammation, secretory IgA imbalance, organ dysfunction, increased gut permeability (leaky gut), H.pylori and more. It uses state of the art PCR and DNA technology. The GI MAP test is most comprehensive and accurate stool test available on the market today. Great for those experiencing ongoing gut complaints including bloating, flatulence, food sensitivities, cramping, GI pain, discomfort, diarrhoea, constipation, IBS, IBD, IBS and more.

The GI360™ is a comprehensive multi-technology stool test specifically developed to provide clinically actionable results, including standardised dysbiosis index and live susceptibility testing, this breakthrough test utilises the widest array of highly validated technologies in one test. The GI360™ Profile utilises new evidence-based DNA mapping technology coupled with growth-based culture and ID by MALDI-TOF, sensitive biochemical assays and microscopy. Tests metabolites found in urine for infections, mould, candida, yeast, clostridia, nutritional deficiencies, dysfunction in metabolism, detoxification pathways, energy pathways, folate metabolism, toxic load, and much more. A great comprehensive and broad test, which tests multiple systems and pathways in the body. Ideal for every age, especially great for those looking to understand their overall health status. DIAGNOSTIC SOLUTIONS OMX TEST (organic metabolomics test)
Is perfect for those who want to assess their overall quality of health and identify imbalances, deficiencies, dysregulation of pathways and much more. It has hundreds of markers which may reveal essential factors about the health of the individual. The OMX™ Organic Metabolomics test in Australia by Designs for Health and Diagnostic Solutions Laboratory evaluates urine metabolites, including organic acids, amino acids, and other key compounds, to assist practitioners in unravelling their patient’s metabolic pathways, revealing their unique Metabolic Signature. KBMO DIAGNOSTICS FIT176 TEST (food inflammation and sensitivity test, with added intestinal health markers)
Tests for immediate and delayed food reactions involving IgG1, IgG2, IgG3, IgG4 and Complement (C3d). The worlds most advanced test for food sensitivities, intolerances and inflammation produced by eating certain foods. Its ideal for those experiencing gut related complaints, fatigue, food sensitivities, autoimmune disease, skin conditions, headaches, migraines and others. Food Sensitivities and related diseases affect at least 100 million people worldwide and cause a variety of illnesses ranging from skin rashes and headaches to chronic intestinal diseases. Food sensitivities may develop for several reasons including imbalanced or inadequate diets, enzyme deficiencies, stress, genetic predisposition, infections and inflammation, chemicals, drugs, environmental pollutants and toxins. Research has shown that these factors may weaken both digestion as well as the protective defences in our intestinal tract allowing allergens to more easily confront our overwhelmed immune system.

A great all in one, designed to test multiple systems and pathways, to identify the root cause of disease. The Nutri-STAT panel is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of important biomarkers involved in the pathophysiology of many complex, chronic and idiopathic conditions. The immune system is greatly impacted by an individual’s dietary patterns, and poor nutritional status can influence the risk of developing immune-mediated diseases. Nutrient deficiency, toxin build up and enzymatic dysfunction are common factors that lead to varying nutrient requirements. This report provides extensive insight into metabolic health, nutritional status, essential fatty acid levels, cardiovascular health, and endocrine function. Which tests all thyroid parameters leaving no stone unturned. MOSAIC DIAGNOSTICS MYCOTOX PROFILE TEST (mould toxicity test)
Mycotoxins are some of the most prevalent toxins in the environment. Mycotoxins are metabolites produced by fungi like mould, which can infest buildings, vehicles, and foodstuffs. A majority of mycotoxin exposures are through food ingestion or airborne exposure. In the European Union, 20% of all grains harvested have been found to be contaminated with mycotoxins. Unfortunately, mycotoxins are resistant to heat and many processing procedures. Fungi are able to grow on almost any surface, especially if the environment is warm and wet. Inner wall materials of buildings, wall paper, fibreglass insulation, ceiling tiles, and drywall (Gyprock) are all good surfaces for fungi to colonise. These fungi then release mycotoxins into the environment which may cause symptoms of many different chronic diseases. Diseases and symptoms that may be linked to mycotoxin exposure include fever, pneumonia-like symptoms, heart disease, rheumatic disease, asthma, sinusitis, memory loss, vision loss, chronic fatigue, skin rashes, depression, ADHD, anxiety, and liver damage.
