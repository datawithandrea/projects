Functional lab testing

Evidence in place of guesswork

Where indicated, specialised testing replaces assumptions with a detailed picture of what's actually happening in your body.

Hormones

DUTCH Test

A comprehensive dried-urine hormone profile covering sex hormones, cortisol rhythm and key metabolites. Especially useful for ongoing hormonal dysfunction, PCOS and cycle irregularities.

Gut

GI-MAP Stool Analysis

PCR/DNA-based testing for pathogens, dysbiosis, inflammation and gut permeability. A strong starting point for chronic gut complaints and IBS.

Environment

Mycotoxin Profile

Screens for mould-related toxins linked to fatigue, sinus issues, skin reactions and cognitive symptoms.

Thyroid

Complete Thyroid Panel

A full thyroid work-up, TSH through to antibodies, for unexplained weight change, fatigue or a family history of thyroid disease.

Additional testing available on indication, including organic acids, food sensitivity, adrenal and genetic panels. Your practitioner will recommend testing only where it changes the treatment plan.
